TEMPLATE: Notice of incomplete application (ECOA / Regulation B)

PURPOSE
The written notice a creditor sends when a credit application is missing
information that the applicant can supply, as the alternative to notifying
the applicant of action taken.

WHEN TO USE WHICH VARIANT (both are in this file)
- Variant A, written notice: the creditor asks for the missing information in
  writing. 12 CFR 1002.9(c)(2).
- Variant B, written notice after an oral request: the creditor first asked
  by phone or in person under 1002.9(c)(3) and the application is still
  incomplete. The written notice must still go out within the original
  30 days.
- Do not use either variant: for a preapproval that counts as an application
  (comment 9(c)(1)-1: the notice requirement does not apply); when the
  creditor has enough information to decide and denies for another reason
  (comment 9(a)(1)-4: "incomplete application" cannot then be the reason);
  or when the creditor chooses to deny for incompleteness, which is an
  adverse action notice under 1002.9(a) (comment 9(a)(1)-3).

SOURCES (retrieved 2026-11-11; pages marked "This version is the current regulation")
- 12 CFR 1002.9 with official interpretations
  https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/9/
- Supplement I, comments to 1002.9
  https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/interp-9/
- 12 CFR Part 1002, Appendix C, Form C-6, Sample Notice of Incomplete
  Application and Request for Additional Information
  https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/c/

REQUIRED ELEMENTS AND WHERE EACH COMES FROM
1. In writing - 1002.9(c)(2): "the creditor shall send a written notice to
   the applicant"
2. The information needed - 1002.9(c)(2): "specifying the information needed"
3. A reasonable period of time to provide it - 1002.9(c)(2): "designating a
   reasonable period of time for the applicant to provide the information".
   Form C-6 states it as a date.
4. The consequence - 1002.9(c)(2): "informing the applicant that failure to
   provide the information requested will result in no further consideration
   being given to the application"
5. Sent within 30 days after receiving the incomplete application -
   1002.9(c)(1). After an oral request, still within that 30-day period -
   1002.9(c)(3) and comment 9(c)(3)-1.
6. Addressed to the primary applicant where one is readily apparent, when
   there is more than one applicant - 1002.9(f)
Creditor's name, address, telephone number, the date and the "Dear
Applicant" wording follow Form C-6. Appendix C, paragraph 5: proper use of
Form C-6 "constitutes full compliance with" 1002.9(c)(2); a creditor may
also design its own form.

LIMITS
- Regulation B does not define "reasonable period of time" in the text read.
  The creditor chooses the date.
- The application reference line, and in Variant B the sentence naming the
  date of the oral request, are not in Form C-6 or 1002.9(c). They are layout
  to identify the file. Delete them if not wanted.
- The duty is the creditor's. Whether a broker or other party sends it for
  the creditor was not researched.
- A creditor that received no more than 150 applications in the preceding
  calendar year may give the notifications in 1002.9 orally (1002.9(d)).
- This covers Regulation B only. TRID, RESPA, HMDA, FCRA, state law and
  investor or agency deadlines are not covered.

# Before sending: timing check

From 12 CFR 1002.9(c) and its official interpretations.

- [ ] Date the incomplete application was received: [DATE APPLICATION RECEIVED]
- [ ] Latest date to send this notice (30 days after receipt): [DATE APPLICATION RECEIVED + 30 DAYS]
- [ ] The missing items are matters the applicant can complete (1002.9(c)(1))
- [ ] The notice names every item needed (1002.9(c)(2))
- [ ] The notice gives a response deadline the creditor considers reasonable (1002.9(c)(2))
- [ ] The notice says failure to provide the information means no further consideration (1002.9(c)(2))
- [ ] More than one applicant: addressed to the primary applicant where one is readily apparent (1002.9(f))
- [ ] Variant B only: an earlier oral request does not extend the 30 days (comment 9(c)(3)-1)

# After sending

- Applicant supplies the information within the designated period: the creditor must take action on the application and notify the applicant under 1002.9(a) (1002.9(c)(2)).
- Applicant does not respond within the designated period: the creditor has no further obligation under 1002.9 (1002.9(c)(2)).
- Information arrives after the period ends: the creditor may require a new application (comment 9(c)(2)-1).
