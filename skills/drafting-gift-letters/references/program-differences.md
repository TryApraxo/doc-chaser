## Comparison

| | Fannie Mae (B3-4.3-04) | Freddie Mac (5501.4(a) item 1) | FHA (4000.1 II.A.4.d.iii(F), II.A.5.c.iii(F)) | VA (26-7 Ch. 4, Topic 4.d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who may give | A relative (spouse, child, other dependent, or anyone related by blood, marriage, adoption or legal guardianship); or a non-relative with a familial relationship (domestic partner or that partner's relative, fiance, former relative, long-standing familial-like or mentorship relationship). Not the builder, developer, real estate agent or other interested party, or an affiliate. A seller who is an acceptable donor and unaffiliated may give | A Related Person, a business owned by one, a trust established by one, or the estate of one. Related Person (Glossary): spouse, child or dependent; related by blood, marriage or adoption; guardian or ward; fiance or fiancee; domestic partner; unrelated individual with close, family-like ties | The Borrower's Family Member; employer or labor union; a close friend with a clearly defined and documented interest in the Borrower; a charitable organization; a governmental agency or public entity with a homeownership assistance program | A donor with no affiliation with the builder, developer, real estate agent or any other interested party. No relationship list in Topic 4.d |
| Who signs the letter | The donor | The donor; for a business, the Related Person owner; for a trust, the trustee; for an estate, the authorized representative | The donor and the Borrower, signed and dated | Not stated in Topic 4.d |
| Amount wording | Actual or maximum dollar amount | Actual or maximum amount | The dollar amount of the Gift | The dollar amount of the gift |
| Repayment statement | Donor's statement that no repayment is expected | Letter establishes that the funds are a gift that does not have to be repaid | Statement that no repayment is required | Donor's statement that no repayment is expected |
| Donor details | Name, address, telephone number, relationship to the borrower | Name; statement of which donor category applies; mailing address and telephone number | Name, address, telephone number; relationship to the Borrower | Name, address, telephone number, relationship to the borrower |
| Extra letter content | Trust or estate: name of the trust or estate account. Donor pooling funds for the minimum down payment: donor certification of 12 months' shared residence, plus proof of shared address | Business donor: statement that the Related Person has an ownership interest in the business | None listed | None listed |
| Transfer evidence | Funds shown in the donor's account or transferred to the borrower: donor's check and borrower's deposit slip; donor's withdrawal slip and borrower's deposit slip; evidence of electronic transfer to the borrower or closing agent; donor's check to the closing agent; settlement statement showing receipt of the donor's check. If not transferred before settlement: electronic transfer, certified, cashier's or other official check to the closing agent | Funds must go directly from the donor's account at a financial institution to the Borrower's account or the settlement or closing agent (earnest money may go to the builder or agent). Evidence: statements from both accounts, canceled gift check, or withdrawal and deposit slips; cashier's check copy or wire confirmation to the closing agent; money transfer apps only when shown bank account to bank account or to the closing agent | Before settlement, one of: donor's bank statement showing the withdrawal, canceled check, or withdrawal receipt, each with evidence of deposit to the Borrower's account; or evidence of electronic transfer to the Borrower's account. At settlement, payment to the settlement agent by electronic transfer, bank certified check, cashier's check or other official bank check. Gift of land: donor's proof of ownership and evidence of title transfer | Funds transferred to the borrower's account, or documented as received by the closing agent at closing: evidence of the borrower's deposit; donor's check or electronic transfer to the closing agent; or the CD showing receipt |
| Occupancy limits | Principal residence or second home. Not investment property | Primary Residence or second home. Not Investment Property Mortgages | Not stated in the section read | Not stated in Topic 4.d |
| Borrower's own funds | Above 80% LTV/CLTV/HCLTV: 5% from the borrower's own funds for a two- to four-unit principal residence or a second home; none for a one-unit principal residence. 80% or less: none | Second home above 80% LTV/TLTV/HTLTV: Down Payment of at least 5% from Borrower personal funds | Gift funds for the Minimum Required Investment must not come from the seller, anyone who financially benefits from the transaction, or anyone they reimburse (II.A.4.d.ii) | Not stated in Topic 4.d |
| Reserves | Gift funds may fund reserves, after any minimum contribution | Section 5501.4 says its requirements apply to all funds used to qualify, including reserves; no reserve bar for gift funds in item 1 | Manual underwriting: surplus gift funds may not be counted as cash Reserves. No matching line in the TOTAL section read | Rental income items in Chapter 4 say gift funds cannot be used to meet reserve requirements |
| Donor's source of funds | Not restricted in the topic beyond donor eligibility | Must come from the donor's account at a financial institution | Cash on Hand is not an acceptable source of donor gift funds | Not stated in Topic 4.d |

## Gifts of equity

| | Fannie Mae (B3-4.3-05) | Freddie Mac (5501.4(a) item 4) | FHA (4000.1 (F)(4)) | VA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who may give | The property seller, who must be an acceptable donor under B3-4.3-04; not treated as an interested party | The property seller, as a Related Person, a business owned by one, a trust established by one, or the estate of one | Only Family Members, on Property being sold to other Family Members | Not addressed in Topic 4.d |
| Letter | Signed gift letter meeting B3-4.3-04 | Same contents as the gift funds letter, worded for a gift of equity | Same contents as the FHA gift letter, signed and dated by donor and Borrower | - |
| Other evidence | Settlement statement listing the gift of equity | Receipt shown on the Settlement/Closing Disclosure Statement | None listed in (F)(4) | - |
| Limits | Principal residence or second home purchases; down payment and closing costs only; not reserves; minimum borrower contribution rules apply | Primary Residence or second home; not reserves; not cash back to the Borrower; second home above 80% needs 5% Borrower personal funds; not Investment Property | None listed in (F)(4) | - |

## Not covered

USDA loans, Fannie Mae HomeReady, Freddie Mac Home Possible and HeritageOne, wedding and graduation gifts (Freddie Mac 5501.4(a) items 2 and 3), grants and agency gifts, lender overlays, and gift tax. "Not stated" means the section cited does not say; another section of the same guide may.
