# Gift Letter (Freddie Mac)

Date: [DATE SIGNED]

To: [LENDER NAME]

Re: Mortgage application of [BORROWER NAME(S)]
Property: [PROPERTY ADDRESS]

I, [DONOR NAME], am giving gift funds of [ACTUAL AMOUNT, OR "up to" THE MAXIMUM AMOUNT, IN FIGURES AND WORDS] to [BORROWER NAME(S)].

**These funds are a gift. They do not have to be repaid.**

**The gift funds are given by (tick one):**

- [ ] A Related Person of the Borrower. Relationship: [RELATIONSHIP]
- [ ] A business owned by a Related Person of the Borrower. Business name: [BUSINESS NAME]. [RELATED PERSON NAME], who is the Borrower's [RELATIONSHIP], has an ownership interest in this business.
- [ ] A trust established by a Related Person of the Borrower. Trust name: [TRUST NAME]. Established by: [RELATED PERSON NAME], the Borrower's [RELATIONSHIP]
- [ ] The estate of a Related Person of the Borrower. Estate of: [RELATED PERSON NAME], the Borrower's [RELATIONSHIP]

**Donor**

- Name: [DONOR NAME, OR NAME OF THE BUSINESS, TRUST OR ESTATE]
- Mailing address: [MAILING ADDRESS OF THE DONOR, BUSINESS, TRUSTEE OR AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE]
- Telephone number: [TELEPHONE OF THE DONOR, BUSINESS, TRUSTEE OR AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE]

Signature: ______________________ Date: __________

Printed name: [NAME OF SIGNER]

Signing as (tick one):

- [ ] Donor
- [ ] Related Person with an ownership interest in the donor business
- [ ] Trustee of the donor trust
- [ ] Authorized representative of the donor estate

## Evidence of receipt for the lender to collect

Not part of the letter. Paraphrased from Section 5501.4(a), item 1. One of:

- [ ] Transfer from the donor's account at a financial institution to the Borrower's account, for example bank statements from both accounts, a canceled gift check, or the donor's withdrawal slip with the Borrower's deposit slip
- [ ] Transfer from the donor's account at a financial institution to the settlement or closing agent, for example a cashier's check copy or a wire confirmation
- [ ] Earnest money paid by the donor directly to the builder or real estate agent: transfer from the donor's account at a financial institution to the deposit holder, for example a canceled gift check, cashier's check copy or wire confirmation
- [ ] Money transfer app or service: acceptable only when the file shows the funds went directly from the donor's bank account to the Borrower's bank account or to the settlement or closing agent

## Note: gift of equity under Freddie Mac

Section 5501.4(a), item 4 requires the same letter contents for a gift of equity (signer rules, donor name and donor category, mailing address and telephone, actual or maximum amount, no repayment, business ownership statement), and receipt must be shown on the Settlement/Closing Disclosure Statement. A gift of equity may not be used for reserves or as cash back to the Borrower. To adapt this letter, replace "gift funds" with "gift of equity" throughout.
