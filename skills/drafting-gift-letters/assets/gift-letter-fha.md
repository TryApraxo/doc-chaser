PURPOSE
A letter the donor and the Borrower sign so the Mortgagee can document gift funds

transmittal "Issued: August 12, 2026", page footers "Last Revised: 8/12/2026".

- II.A.4.d.iii(F) Gifts (Personal and Equity) (TOTAL), PDF pages 258-260
- II.A.5.c.iii(F) Gifts (Personal and Equity) (Manual), PDF pages 337-339

The section heading "Asset Requirements (TOTAL)" and "(Manual)" carries the

REQUIRED ELEMENTS AND WHERE EACH COMES FROM
1. Signed and dated by the donor AND the Borrower
   - (F)(3) Required Documentation: gift letter "signed and dated by the donor
     and Borrower"
2. Donor's name, address and telephone number - (F)(3), first bullet
3. Donor's relationship to the Borrower - (F)(3), second bullet
4. Dollar amount of the Gift - (F)(3), third bullet
5. Statement that no repayment is required - (F)(3), fourth bullet
The same four contents and both signatures are required for a gift of equity
((F)(4)(b)).

WHO MAY GIVE ((F)(2)(a) Acceptable Sources of Gifts Funds)
The Borrower's Family Member; the Borrower's employer or labor union; a close
friend with a clearly defined and documented interest in the Borrower; a
charitable organization; a governmental agency or public entity with a
homeownership assistance program for low- or moderate-income families or
first-time homebuyers. Gift of equity: only Family Members, on Property being
sold to other Family Members ((F)(4)(a)).

LIMITS
- The Handbook does not use "actual or maximum" wording; it says "the dollar
  amount of the Gift". State the real amount.
- "Cash on Hand is not an acceptable source of donor gift funds" ((F)(2), Donor's
  Source of Funds).
- Manual underwriting only: "Surplus gift funds may not be considered as cash
  Reserves" (II.A.5.c.iii(F)(2)(b)). The TOTAL section read has no matching line.
- Gift funds used for the Minimum Required Investment must not come from the
  seller, anyone who financially benefits from the transaction, or anyone
  reimbursed by them (II.A.4.d.ii(B)).
- Not covered here: gifts from charitable organizations and governmental
  entities beyond the donor list above, Title I, HECM, and lender overlays.
- The donor-type tick boxes and the transfer checklist below restate the
  Handbook; they are not extra Handbook letter requirements.
- The Handbook is revised often. Confirm the current edition before use.

# Gift Letter (FHA)

Date: [DATE SIGNED]

To: [MORTGAGEE NAME]

Re: FHA loan application of [BORROWER NAME(S)]
Property: [PROPERTY ADDRESS]

I, [DONOR NAME], am giving a Gift of $[GIFT AMOUNT IN FIGURES] ([GIFT AMOUNT IN WORDS] dollars) to [BORROWER NAME(S)].

**Donor's relationship to the Borrower:** [RELATIONSHIP]

The donor is (tick one):

- [ ] The Borrower's Family Member
- [ ] The Borrower's employer or labor union
- [ ] A close friend with a clearly defined and documented interest in the Borrower
- [ ] A charitable organization
- [ ] A governmental agency or public entity with a homeownership assistance program

**No repayment of this Gift is required.**

## Transfer documentation for the Mortgagee to collect

Not part of the letter. From Handbook 4000.1 II.A.4.d.iii(F)(3) and II.A.5.c.iii(F)(3), "Documenting the Transfer of Gifts".

**Gift verified before settlement - one of:**

- [ ] The donor's bank statement showing the withdrawal, and evidence of the deposit into the Borrower's account
- [ ] A copy of the donor's canceled check, and evidence of deposit into the Borrower's account
- [ ] A copy of the donor's withdrawal receipt, and evidence of deposit into the Borrower's account
- [ ] Evidence of the electronic transfer of funds from the donor's account to the Borrower's account

**Gift verified at settlement - one of the following, evidencing payment to the settlement agent:**

- [ ] Evidence of electronic transfer of funds from the donor's account
- [ ] Bank certified check
- [ ] Cashier's check
- [ ] Other official bank check

**Gift of land:**

- [ ] Proof of ownership by the donor
- [ ] Evidence of the transfer of title to the Borrower

Whenever the funds arrive, the Mortgagee must be able to make a reasonable determination that the gift funds did not come from an unacceptable source.
