# Gift Letter (VA)

Date: [DATE SIGNED]

To: [LENDER NAME]

Re: VA loan application of [BORROWER NAME(S)]
Property: [PROPERTY ADDRESS]

I, [DONOR NAME], am giving a gift of $[GIFT AMOUNT IN FIGURES] ([GIFT AMOUNT IN WORDS] dollars) to [BORROWER NAME(S)].

**My relationship to the borrower:** [RELATIONSHIP]

**This is a gift. No repayment is expected.**

I have no affiliation with the builder, the developer, the real estate agent, or any other interested party to this transaction.

**Donor**

- Name: [DONOR NAME]
- Address: [DONOR ADDRESS]
- Telephone number: [DONOR TELEPHONE]

Donor signature: ______________________ Date: __________

Printed name: [DONOR NAME]

---

## Transfer documentation for the lender to collect

Not part of the letter. From VA Pamphlet 26-7, Chapter 4, Topic 4.d.

The lender must verify that sufficient funds to cover the gift have been transferred to the borrower's account, or will be documented as received by the closing agent at the time of closing. Acceptable documentation includes:

- [ ] Evidence of the borrower's deposit
- [ ] A copy of the donor's funds by check or electronic transfer to the closing agent
- [ ] The Closing Disclosure showing receipt of the donor's funds
