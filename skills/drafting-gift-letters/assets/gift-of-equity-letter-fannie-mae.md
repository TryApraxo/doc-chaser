PURPOSE
A letter the seller-donor signs when the seller of the property gives the

REQUIRED ELEMENTS AND WHERE EACH COMES FROM
B3-4.3-05, Documentation Requirements: the loan file must hold (a) a signed
gift letter that follows B3-4.3-04 and (b) the settlement statement listing
the gift of equity.
Letter contents, from B3-4.3-04, Documentation Requirements:
1. Signed by the donor
2. Actual or maximum dollar amount of the gift
3. The donor's statement that no repayment is expected
4. The donor's name, address, telephone number and relationship to the
   borrower
5. Trust or estate of an acceptable donor: letter signed by the donor and
   listing the name of the trust or the estate account

WHO MAY GIVE
The seller of the property, who must be an acceptable donor under B3-4.3-04:
a relative (spouse, child or other dependent, or anyone related by blood,
marriage, adoption or legal guardianship), or a non-relative with a familial
relationship (domestic partner or that partner's relative, a person engaged
to marry the borrower, a former relative, or a person with a long-standing
familial-like or mentorship relationship). An acceptable donor of a gift of
equity is not treated as an interested party (B3-4.3-05).

LIMITS
- Principal residence and second home purchase transactions only (B3-4.3-05).
- May fund all or part of the down payment and closing costs, including
  prepaid items. May NOT be used toward financial reserves (B3-4.3-05).
- The minimum borrower contribution rules for gifts also apply (B3-4.3-05,
  referring to B3-4.3-04): above 80% LTV/CLTV/HCLTV, a two- to four-unit
  principal residence or a second home needs 5% from the borrower's own funds.
- The "seller of the property" line and the property line identify the gift
  as a gift of equity; B3-4.3-05 defines it as a gift from the seller's
  equity, transferred as a credit in the transaction. They are not separately
  listed letter contents.

# Gift of Equity Letter (Fannie Mae)

Date: [DATE SIGNED]

To: [LENDER NAME]

Re: Loan application of [BORROWER NAME(S)]
Property being purchased: [PROPERTY ADDRESS]

I, [DONOR NAME], am the seller of the property above. I am giving [BORROWER NAME(S)] a gift of equity of [ACTUAL AMOUNT, OR "up to" THE MAXIMUM AMOUNT, IN FIGURES AND WORDS], to be given as a credit to the buyer in this purchase.

**My relationship to the borrower:** [RELATIONSHIP]

**This is a gift. No repayment is expected.**

[DELETE UNLESS THE GIFT IS FROM A TRUST OR ESTATE] Name of the trust or estate account: [TRUST NAME OR ESTATE ACCOUNT NAME]

**Donor**

- Name: [DONOR NAME]
- Address: [DONOR ADDRESS]
- Telephone number: [DONOR TELEPHONE]

Donor signature: ______________________ Date: __________

Printed name: [DONOR NAME]
