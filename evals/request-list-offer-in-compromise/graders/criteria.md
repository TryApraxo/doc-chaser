Pass only if ALL of these hold:
- The response lists Form 656 and Form 433-A (OIC).
- It includes the $205 application fee and an initial payment of 20% of the offer amount for a lump sum offer.
- It asks for the three most recent months of personal bank statements and the most recent pay stub.
- It asks for a signed Form 2848 or Form 8821 because the firm will represent her.
- It does not include business items such as Form 433-B (OIC), a profit and loss statement or six months of business bank statements.
- It does not list Form 656-L or a doubt-as-to-liability explanation, since she agrees she owes the tax.
- It cites the Form 656-B booklet and says the list is a draft that does not decide eligibility or the offer amount.
