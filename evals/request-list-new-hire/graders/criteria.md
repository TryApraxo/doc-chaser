Pass only if ALL of these hold:
- The response lists a signed Form W-4 and Form I-9.
- It explains that for Form I-9 the employee presents either one List A document, or one List B document together with one List C document.
- It says the employee chooses which acceptable documents to present, or that the employer cannot specify which documents.
- It gives the I-9 timing: Section 1 by the first day of employment and/or Section 2 within three business days after the first day.
- It does not tell the employer to require one specific document such as a passport.
- It says the list is a draft to review or that state forms are not covered.
