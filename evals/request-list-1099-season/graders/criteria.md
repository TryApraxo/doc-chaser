Pass only if ALL of these hold:
- The response asks for a Form W-9 (or the taxpayer identification number) for each payee.
- It asks for total payments per non-employee and states the $2,000 threshold for payments in 2026, not $600 for non-employee compensation.
- It asks how each payment was made and explains that credit card or payment-app (third-party network) payments are not reported on Form 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC by the business.
- It includes rent paid to the landlord (Form 1099-MISC).
- It includes payments to the law firm, distinguishing legal fees from settlement or gross proceeds, and gives the $600 threshold for attorney gross proceeds.
- It cites IRS Publication 1099 or the Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC.
