Pass only if ALL of these hold:
- The response is a mortgage needs list for a self-employed borrower.
- It asks for signed personal federal tax returns for the past two years AND signed business federal tax returns for the past two years.
- It asks for statements for the business account the down payment funds will come from.
- It names the Fannie Mae Selling Guide as the source of the items.
- It says the list is a draft for the loan officer to review, or that lender requirements may add conditions.
- It does not state that the borrower qualifies or is approved.
